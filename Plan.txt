# AI AGENT EXECUTION PLAN

## Visual Financial Simulator

You are building a production-quality financial simulation application.

Do not attempt to build the entire product at once.

Work through the phases below in order.

Each phase has:

* Objective
* Tasks
* Deliverables
* Validation
* Definition of done

Do not move to the next phase until the current phase passes its validation requirements.

---

# PHASE 0 — UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCT

## Objective

Before writing code, understand the product architecture.

The product is:

> A visual business-model builder where users connect business assumptions into a graph, simulate the business over time, run scenarios and Monte Carlo simulations, and understand the resulting financial outcomes.

The core loop is:

```text
DEFINE BUSINESS
      ↓
BUILD MODEL
      ↓
SIMULATE
      ↓
UNDERSTAND
      ↓
CHANGE ASSUMPTIONS
      ↓
SIMULATE AGAIN
```

The application should feel like:

```text
n8n
+
Figma
+
financial simulator
+
AI copilot
```

---

## Tasks

1. Inspect the repository.
2. Identify existing framework and dependencies.
3. Identify existing UI components.
4. Identify existing authentication.
5. Identify existing database.
6. Identify deployment configuration.

8. Identify existing AI integrations.
9. Identify existing environment variables.



## Add unit handling

```text

credits
percent
months
years
```

Prevent invalid calculations where possible.

Example:

```text

```


# AGENT BEHAVIOR RULES

Throughout the entire implementation:

1. Do not rewrite working infrastructure without a reason.
2. Do not add dependencies unnecessarily.
3. Do not build mock functionality and leave it disconnected.
4. Do not hardcode simulation results.
5. Do not let AI invent financial outputs.
6. Do not use eval.
7. Do not block the main browser thread with Monte Carlo.
8. Do not hide assumptions.
9. Do not hide formulas.
10. Do not silently repair invalid financial models.
11. Do not create fake metrics.
12. Do not claim a feature is complete until it works end-to-end.
13. Prefer simple implementations.
14. Keep simulation logic independent of UI.
15. Keep AI logic independent of simulation calculations.
16. Write tests for financial formulas.
17. Use deterministic seeds.
18. Keep the application Vercel-friendly.
19. Optimize only after measuring.
20. When uncertain, preserve correctness over visual polish.
